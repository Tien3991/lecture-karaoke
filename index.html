Lecture Karaoke

Plays a lecture video or audio recording with live karaoke subtitles from its transcript. Lectures come from this device or your Dropbox; transcripts come from your private GitHub repository, where new ones get transcribed.

On this device

On iPhone and iPad, pick files with Choose File (the Files app), not the photo library. Add the app to the Home Screen to watch full screen.

You can also drop both files here.

      The buttons beside play/pause on the lock screen. A phone shows one pair:

      An iPhone skips 15 seconds (it draws the buttons that way).

      The sound plays a second time, silently, along with the video, so it goes on at once when the screen locks or you switch apps, and when you come back. Uses twice the data while you watch.

        Delete from Dropbox?

          Dropbox keeps deleted files for at least 30 days: you can restore them from Deleted files on dropbox.com. Their transcripts stay on GitHub, so a file put back finds its transcript again.
