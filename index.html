Lecture Karaoke

Plays a lecture video with live karaoke subtitles from its transcript. Your files stay on this device; nothing is uploaded.

On iPhone and iPad, pick files with Choose File (the Files app), not the photo library. Add the app to the Home Screen to watch full screen.
